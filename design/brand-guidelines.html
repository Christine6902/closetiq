01 — OUR STORY

A personal stylist in your pocket

ClosetIQ turns the clothes you already own into a wardrobe that thinks. Photograph each piece, set your style and let ClosetIQ compose complete outfits for the day's weather, your plans and your taste — so the question is never "what do I wear?" again.

Where most wardrobe apps feel like inventory software, ClosetIQ is built to feel like a boutique stylist: considered, warm and quietly premium. It helps you wear more of what you own, pack smarter for travel, and dress with confidence for every occasion.

02 — LOGO

The mark

The ClosetIQ lockup pairs a fine-line hanger-in-frame symbol with a serif wordmark and the tagline. The hanger-in-frame is the reusable icon — use it alone for app icons, favicons and avatars.

Primary lockup · on cream / light surfaces
On charcoal — supplied artwork is light-ground; a dedicated reversed lockup should be produced by the designer.
On gold — use the charcoal mark; confirm legibility at final sizes.

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the "IQ" letters on all sides. Never place type or graphics inside this zone. Minimum size: 120px wide for the full lockup; 24px for the hanger icon alone.

Misuse

✗ Don't place on an unapproved colour

✗ Don't reduce opacity

✗ Don't rotate or distort

✗ Don't add shadows, glows or recolour

03 — COLOUR

Palette

Every colour is drawn from the logo: a warm cream ground, antique-gold accent and charcoal. Gold is a decorative accent — it is reserved for fills, borders and the logo echo. For gold text, use the darker Accent Strong, which meets accessibility contrast on cream.

04 — TYPOGRAPHY

Type system

Cormorant Garamond carries the brand's editorial elegance at display sizes; Jost — a geometric sans echoing the tagline's spaced caps — handles everything at reading and UI size. The pairing mirrors the logo: serif wordmark, geometric-caps strapline.

Dress with intention.

05 — TONE OF VOICE

How ClosetIQ speaks

Considered

Like a stylist who knows your taste. We make a clear recommendation rather than listing every option.

Warm

Encouraging, never preachy. We build confidence; we don't shame a wardrobe or a choice.

Effortless

Plain, calm language. Short sentences. The product removes friction, and so does the copy.

Quietly premium

Elegant, not flashy. No exclamation-mark hype, no emoji clutter — restraint signals quality.

Write this

"Cooler today — we've added your wool coat to the look."

Not this

"BRR! It's freezing!! Don't forget a jacket!!! 🥶🧥"

Write this

"Three pieces you haven't worn in a while pair well for tonight."

Not this

"You NEVER wear these items — stop wasting your money!"
